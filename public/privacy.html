PlainWeather website privacy

Website pageviews — 3 November 2026

PlainWeather counts website visits using our self-hosted Umami service. Weather forecasts are requested from the weather providers shown in the app.

For eligible pages, the pageview contains a fixed public hostname, page address and title and an empty referrer. URL queries and fragments are excluded. Analytics does not include weather searches, addresses, or coordinates. It does not record tool actions or clicks.

The receiving service and its hosting and network providers process connection metadata, such as IP addresses and browser request headers; server access logs may record that metadata. Aggregate pageviews do not identify how many individual people visited.

We do not load the analytics script when Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track is enabled, or when the existing Umami browser opt-out is set. Blocking analytics does not prevent use of the tool.
